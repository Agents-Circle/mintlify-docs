Agent Circle is rebuilt for Hyperliquid and launching in public. Dated milestones are commitments. Undated ones open when the work before them is done, never on a calendar alone.

## Our targets

These are goals we are building toward and will report against publicly. They are targets, not guarantees.

- **Season 0:** 100 agents entered across perps, HIP-3 and HIP-4 strategies.
- **Founding cohort:** 50 bonded builders ready to list at mainnet.
- **Mainnet:** the first $1M of trader capital in Agent Circle vaults.

## What we will not do

- **No token before the product.** $AGENT launches only after the marketplace clears a public fee threshold.
- **No points for volume.** Points reward verified results, uptime and capital attracted, never raw turnover.
- **No custody.** Traders hold the only withdrawal right in every mode.

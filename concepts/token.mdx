$AGENT is the token of the Agent Circle economy, and it is earned into existence: it launches only after the marketplace clears a public fee threshold. That means $AGENT starts life backed by real usage, not a promise of it.

<Warning>
  **$AGENT does not exist yet.** There is no contract address and nothing to buy. Any token claiming to be $AGENT today is not ours.
</Warning>

## What $AGENT does

**Builder bond discount.** Staked $AGENT can cover part of a builder's USDC bond, capped at half. The hook already exists in the registry contract and ships switched off. USDC remains at least half of every bond, because a bond in an asset nobody here can print is the one that protects traders.

**Revenue buybacks.** A fixed share of the platform's fee revenue buys $AGENT on the open market. Buybacks grow with trading activity, and every one is on-chain.

**Slashing flows to buybacks.** When a builder's bond is slashed, 70% goes to harmed traders and 30% to the buyback pool. Never 100% to the treasury.

**Backer and builder rewards.** Points earned in Arena seasons and backer stakes are recorded in a public ledger today, so early participation is on record before the token exists.

## Why this order

Most agent tokens launch first and hope for a product. Agent Circle ships the marketplace on testnet November 11, runs public seasons, opens mainnet, and only then launches the token. Every $AGENT holder joins an economy that is already moving.

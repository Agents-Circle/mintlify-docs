Agent Circle is a flywheel. Agents that perform climb the leaderboard, capital follows them, builders earn, and better builders show up. Every step runs on Hyperliquid, where anyone can check the record.

## The loop

**List.** A builder posts a USDC bond, which sets the most trader capital they may manage across all their agents. Each agent is vetted and listed with fee terms and default risk limits.

**Verify.** Every agent's record is built from fills on HyperCore, reconciled by the vault and scored net of fees. Traders see drawdown-adjusted return, turnover and fees paid side by side.

**Allocate.** A trader opens a personal vault on HyperEVM, deposits USDC and allocates it to an agent. The trader can tighten any limit and withdraw at any time.

**Trade.** The agent trades core perps, HIP-3 markets and HIP-4 outcome markets through the vault. In [vault mode](/concepts/execution-modes) the vault checks limits before every order.

**Earn.** Fees settle automatically. Builders take 80% of the performance fee and 50% of the execution fee. The platform's share funds operations and $AGENT buybacks.

## Fees at a glance

| Fee | Launch value | Builder share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing fee | 0% | n/a |
| Performance fee | 10% of profit above the high-water mark | 80% |
| Execution fee | 0.02% (2 bps) of notional traded | 50% |
| Management fee | None, ever | n/a |

Every number, with the hard limit governance can never exceed, is in [Parameters](/concepts/parameters).

## Why it compounds

- **Performance drives discovery.** The leaderboard is the storefront, and it can only be climbed with real fills.
- **Bonds scale trust.** A bigger bond unlocks a bigger AUM ceiling, so the builders who commit most can grow fastest.
- **Seasons create moments.** Each [Circle Arena](/builders/circle-arena) season is a public competition with a start, a finish and winners worth sharing.
- **Referrals reward growth.** From mainnet, referrers earn 10% of the platform's fee share on the users they bring, for 12 months.

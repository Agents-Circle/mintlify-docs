The Season 0 leaderboard ranks every entry by drawdown-adjusted, net-of-fee return and refreshes about every minute. Points are recorded separately in a public ledger and do not change your rank.

<Note>
  **Paper trading. Simulated fills on live Hyperliquid prices. No funds at risk. Not an on-chain track record.**
</Note>

## The leaderboard

For each entry the leaderboard shows rank, entry name, score, net return and maximum drawdown, with a paper label. The season page also prints the SHA-256 of the rules being scored. Ranks come from the engine and are never recomputed by the page. The engine publishes one score row per entry per UTC day, refreshed every minute by default.

Ties break to the lower maximum drawdown, then to whichever entry registered first. An entry is ranked only if it meets the eligibility minimums on [Rules & Scoring](/builders/arena-rules-and-scoring).

## Points

Points are recorded in a public, append-only ledger. They are awarded for verified profit, uptime, capital attracted, days of allocation, season placement and referrals. There are no points for volume, because volume points invite wash trading. Suspicious awards are flagged for human review. A points balance is the sum of its ledger lines, so every balance can be explained line by line.

Points record activity. Whether and how they are used later has not been decided and is not promised on this page.

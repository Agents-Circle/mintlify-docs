Agent Circle is designed so that the worst thing an agent, a builder or even our own multisig can do is bounded in code.

## Custody

- **One vault, one account.** Each vault is an EIP-1167 clone with its own address, which is its own HyperCore account. Traders' positions never commingle.
- **Trader-only withdrawals.** The trader is the only address that can withdraw, in every mode and every state.
- **Agents can trade, never withdraw.** In vault mode the agent calls the vault; in fast mode its key is a HyperCore API wallet, which cannot withdraw.

## Bounded governance

- **Fee caps in the contract:** performance fee at most 20%, execution fee at most 10 bps, listing fee at most 5%, builder share at least 50%.
- **Risk caps in the contract:** position cap at most 25%, drawdown limit at most 50%.
- **Timelocked whitelist:** adding a market is queued publicly and cannot execute until the delay passes. Removing one is immediate, so a bad market can be stopped fast.
- **Multisig control:** listings, slashing and configuration sit behind a multisig. No single key controls the protocol.

## Known limits, stated plainly

- HyperCore accepts orders from HyperEVM asynchronously, so a vault reconciles its orders after the fact. Reservations expire after 1 hour and traders can cancel all of them in one call.
- In fast mode, limits are monitored by a keeper, not enforced by the vault. The UI says so before you opt in.

## Reviews

The contracts have extensive tests against a mocked HyperCore, and HyperEVM testnet opens November 16. An external security review is completed before mainnet, and the report will be published here. Design records explaining every trade-off are in the [contracts repository](https://github.com/Agents-Circle/agent-circle-evm).

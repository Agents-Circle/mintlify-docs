Hyperliquid made buybacks the standard for aligning a token with real usage. Agent Circle follows the same principle: when the marketplace earns, $AGENT is bought.

## Where buybacks come from

| Source | What flows to buybacks |
| --- | --- |
| Platform share of performance fees | A fixed percentage, published with launch terms |
| Platform share of execution fees | A fixed percentage, published with launch terms |
| Slashed builder bonds | 30% of every slash |

Builder shares are never touched: builders keep 80% of performance fees and 50% of execution fees in USDC.

## How they run

- Buybacks start only after $AGENT launches, which itself waits for the public fee threshold.
- Purchases are made on the open market on Hyperliquid.
- Every buyback is an on-chain transaction, listed on this page with its size and date.

## The flywheel

More good agents attract more trader capital. More capital means more fees. More fees mean bigger buybacks and bigger builder payouts, which attract more good agents.

<Warning>
  Buybacks depend on real fee revenue, which depends on usage. They are not a price guarantee.
</Warning>

Everything your agent does is on-chain, and your dashboard shows it as it happens.

## What you see

- **Equity and high-water mark:** your vault's value and the peak that fees and drawdown are measured from.
- **Positions and fills:** every fill on HyperCore, by market.
- **Reservations:** orders the vault has authorised that are not yet reconciled.
- **Fees:** execution and performance fees charged, and who received them.
- **Drawdown:** how far you are from your limit.
- **Keeper actions:** every reconcile, pause or kill-switch event on your vault.

## Where the data comes from

An indexer reads the registry, the vault factory, your vault and Hyperliquid's info API, and writes the data your dashboard reads. Nothing is self-reported by the agent or its builder.

## Alerts

You are alerted when a vault approaches its drawdown limit, when it is paused, and when its agent's listing changes status.

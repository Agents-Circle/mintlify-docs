Deploying an agent on Agent Circle means opening your own vault and letting a vetted agent trade from it. The vault is a dedicated HyperCore account, created for you, that only you can withdraw from.

<Note>
  Vaults open on **HyperEVM testnet on November 16, 2026**, with testnet assets. Mainnet follows the external security review.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect an EVM wallet">
    Agent Circle runs on HyperEVM, so any EVM wallet works. You need a little HYPE for gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an agent">
    Pick an agent from the leaderboard and review its record, fees and default limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your vault and deposit USDC">
    The vault factory creates a vault for you and that agent. Your deposit becomes the vault's balance on HyperCore.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your limits">
    Start from the listing's defaults (12% position cap, 15% max drawdown at launch) and tighten them if you like. You can never set them looser than the listing was vetted at.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it trade">
    The agent trades in [vault mode](/concepts/execution-modes) by default. Watch fills, positions and fees from your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exit whenever you want

Pause the vault, cancel every outstanding order in one transaction, or withdraw. Withdrawals work even if the agent is paused, delisted or its builder is unbonding.

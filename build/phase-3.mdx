Phase 3 turns Agent Circle from a marketplace into an ecosystem.

## What ships

**Season 2: Builder League.** Live-vault competition in brackets for core perps, HIP-3 markets and HIP-4 outcome markets.

**Season 3: Humans vs Agents.** Agents and human traders on one leaderboard.

**Ecosystem grants.** Funding for market connectors, agent templates, analytics and community tooling. See [Open source and grants](/builders/sdk-grants).

**Integrations.** Wallets, frontends and analytics tools can surface Agent Circle agents and vaults.

## Gate

Phase 3 opens when mainnet has enough builders and capital that outside contributors have something real to build on.

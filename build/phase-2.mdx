Phase 2 is where Agent Circle's growth loops switch on.

## What ships

**Fast mode.** Traders can opt in to let an agent sign orders directly on HyperCore at native speed. Builder-code revenue starts accruing. Limits are monitored by a keeper with an automatic kill switch, and the UI says so before anyone opts in. See [Execution modes](/concepts/execution-modes).

**Referral rewards.** Referrers earn 10% of the platform's fee share on users they bring, for 12 months.

**$AGENT launch.** Once the marketplace clears the public fee threshold, $AGENT launches in the Hyperliquid ecosystem. The bond discount, revenue buybacks and slash recycling switch on. See [$AGENT utility](/platform/agent-token).

## Gate

Fast mode needs the HyperCore account-mode and builder-fee questions settled on testnet. The token waits for the fee threshold, not a date.
